# The navigable prototype

The protocol asks for a navigable prototype tested with two real businesses before `apps/editor`
is written. This is it.

**What is different from the mockups:** in `docs/design/mockups/`, screen 07 shows three drawn
pictures of a made-up barbershop. Here it shows **the three real variants of the business sitting
in front of you**, generated by the phase 0 renderer from a document written by hand.

The reason is what each version lets you learn. Looking at a drawing, an owner answers "would you
like something like this?", and everyone says yes. Looking at their own name, their own services
and their own street, they answer "would you publish this?", which is the promise the dossier
makes.

## How the test is walked

1. `docs/design/mockups/01-question-1-business-name.html` through `06-generating.html`, clicking
   `Siguiente`. The questionnaire is static: it measures whether the questions are understood,
   not what is typed.
2. Then **this business's** `07-tres-variantes.html`, which is where the real sites are.
3. `Usar esta` continues to `docs/design/mockups/08-editor.html`. The editor does not exist; the
   screen is there so the journey does not stop dead.

`guion-de-prueba.md` is read before doing any of this.

## How the sites are rebuilt

One command each, from the repository root:

    pnpm site:sample docs/design/prototype/<negocio>/variante-1.json docs/design/prototype/<negocio>/sitio-1

The documents deliberately live here and not in `fixtures/documents/`: they are not golden
corpus, they carry a real business's public data, and they will change as the test teaches us
things.

The screenshots in `vista-*.png` are taken at 1280 from the built sites. They exist because
Chrome will not render a `file://` page inside an `iframe` of another `file://` page, so a live
embed would have shown an empty box on the day.

## What this prototype does and does not measure

**It measures:** whether the five questions are understood, whether people believe the
suggestions in question 3 are already ticked, whether the moment of the three variants produces
a decision or paralysis, and — the one that matters — whether an owner would publish what the
generator produced with their own content in it.

**It does not measure:**
- **the photos.** The bank of ADR 0011 does not exist; every image is a placeholder that says so;
- **the colours.** The palette comes from the logo in the real product. Neither business gave us
  a logo, so it was chosen by hand, knowing which business it was — exactly what the generator
  will not know. The facilitator does not ask about colour;
- **the editor.** It does not exist. Anything past `Usar esta` is a drawing.

Every one of these, and the rest of what building this surfaced, is recorded in
`../REVIEW.md`, under "What building the prototype surfaced".
