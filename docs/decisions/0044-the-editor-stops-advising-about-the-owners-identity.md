# 0044 — The editor stops advising about the owner's identity

**Status:** proposed · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Requested by:** David, 17 de octubre de 2026, al
abrir el sprint 24, en sus propias palabras: «**Elimina el texto** "Las webs de negocios suelen
identificar a su titular. Si tienes dudas, consúltalo con tu gestoría."» · **Recorded by development
at his instruction, with the standing note that development does not sign.** · **Needs the signature
of the CEO**, who accepted [ADR 0019](0019-the-footer-offers-the-owners-details.md) on 28 September
2026 · **Amends:** ADR 0019 · **Touches:** `apps/editor`, `packages/catalog`

## Context

ADR 0019 built the footer as «offered, never required», and put one sentence in the editor to say so.
That sentence is not an implementation detail of that decision — **the ADR says the wording is part
of the decision**, and quotes it in full:

> **The warning claims nothing about the law.** Its wording **is part of this decision, not a detail
> of the implementation**:
>
> > «Las webs de negocios suelen identificar a su titular. Si tienes dudas, consúltalo con tu
> > gestoría.»

So removing it cannot be a tidy-up. It is a decision about the same thing ADR 0019 decided, taken by
the owner of the product, and it needs the signature of whoever signed the first one.

**ADR 0019 anticipated a change here, and this is not the change it anticipated.** Its first
reopening condition reads: «The CEO decides Retorika should take a position on compliance… **Then the
warning's wording is the first thing to change, and it stops being a warning.**» That describes
Retorika saying *more*. This says *nothing at all*.

## Decision

### 1. The sentence goes, and nothing replaces it

`editor.footerIdentity` is removed from the locale, with its only reader and the `footerNeedsIdentity`
condition behind it. The editor's hint strip keeps the download error, the photograph error, the
placeholder count and the editing hint. **The product no longer mentions, anywhere, that a business
website conventionally identifies its owner.**

### 2. The four fields stay, offered and optional, and say so in one word

`FOOTER_SLOTS` is untouched: «Titular», «NIF o CIF», «Domicilio» and «Correo de contacto» are still
there, still `min: 0`, still empty by default, still filled from the fields panel or not at all.

What changes is that each of the four labels now ends in **«(opcional)»**. That sentence was the only
place in the product that said these four fields exist and may be left empty, and the word carries the
second half of it — in the panel where they are filled, as the input's own accessible name, so a
screen reader says it too. It is true rather than reassuring: `min: 0` is what makes it so, and a test
asserts the word marks exactly the slots the preset admits zero of.

**«(opcional)» is a qualifier, not a renaming.** The details are still named the way the person
filling them in would name them — «NIF o CIF» and not «Identificación fiscal» — which is what ADR
0019 decided about the words themselves and what this does not reopen.

### 3. Retorika still takes no position on compliance, and now says nothing at all

ADR 0019's position was *«says what is usual and where to ask, nothing more»*. The position after this
is **silence**. That is a different thing, and it is worth writing down as what it is rather than as a
smaller version of the first.

### 4. The context this accepts, named with its article

**Article 10 of the LSSI-CE** (Ley 34/2002, de servicios de la sociedad de la información y de
comercio electrónico) obliges a provider of information-society services to make available, by
permanent, easy, direct and free electronic means, among other things its name or company name, its
tax identification number and its address.

Nothing in this ADR changes what a published Retorika site contains: the fields are still there and
still fillable, and a site that carries them carries them. What changes is that **the product stops
reminding anybody that they exist**. An owner who does not know about article 10 is now less likely to
find out from us.

**That risk moves to the client**, and this clause is the record that it moved on purpose and on whose
instruction. It is not a legal opinion and does not become one: Retorika takes no position on
compliance, which is ADR 0019's decision and stands.

## What is preserved

- **The footer is still generated by default**, so every site still identifies its business from the
  first render (ADR 0019's own consequence).
- **Offered, never required.** No field becomes mandatory, nothing blocks, and the ZIP still downloads
  with all four empty. The download refuses for exactly one thing, a destination that goes nowhere.
- **Nothing reaches the published page either way.** The advisory was editor-only; so is its absence.
- **The words themselves**, which ADR 0019 chose for being the words a person would use.

## What is lost, and it is named rather than glossed

- **The only sentence in the product that explained why the footer asks for a tax number.** Somebody
  opening «Pie de página» for the first time now meets four optional fields and no reason for them.
  «(opcional)» says they may be left empty; it does not say why they are offered.
- **A cheap warning.** It cost one string and one memo, it blocked nothing, and it was the kind of
  thing that is easier to keep than to re-add. Development's position, recorded because an ADR is
  where a disagreement belongs: the sentence was worth its cost. **David's decision is the one
  implemented**, and this bullet exists so nobody later reads the removal as something nobody
  questioned.
- **A reopening condition of ADR 0019 is spent without being met.** The wording changed, and not
  because the CEO decided to take a position.

## What this does not decide

- **Whether Retorika should take a position on compliance.** Still ADR 0019's answer: no. That
  question is still the CEO's, and this ADR makes it no easier to ask.
- **Whether the fields should be required.** They stay optional.
- **Anything about the terms of use.** ADR 0019 pointed at protocol Part 15 for what comes before the
  first euro, and that pointer still points there.
- **Whether a future screen should explain the footer's fields.** If somebody asks «why is it asking
  me for a NIF», the answer would be a sentence somewhere — and this ADR is the reason there is none.

## Consequences

- `apps/editor`: one locale key, one reader and one `useMemo` removed; the `FOOTER_IDENTITY_SLOTS`
  import goes with them. `FOOTER_ID` stays — it is still what makes the footer unconvertible.
- `packages/catalog`: four labels gain «(opcional)». `FOOTER_IDENTITY_SLOTS` keeps its application
  consumer in the shape of the test that pins the four, and the preset's own `min: 0` is what the word
  is checked against.
- Two comments stopped being true the moment the warning left — `footer.ts`'s «gets a warning in the
  editor» and a test named for «the four details the editor warns about» — and both are corrected in
  the same change rather than left to be found later.
- No published byte changes, so no golden moves.
- The record is `docs/design/REVIEW.md`, sprint 24.

## What would reopen this

- **The CEO declines to sign it.** Then the sentence comes back, and the pull request that removed it
  is closed rather than merged — which is why that pull request waits for the signature instead of
  merging ahead of it.
- **An owner asks why the footer wants their tax number**, which is the question the removed sentence
  answered before it was asked. The answer would be new copy, and it would be a new decision about how
  much Retorika says.
- **The CEO decides Retorika should take a position on compliance**, which is ADR 0019's own first
  condition and is not spent by this: it is still available, and taking it would overwrite this
  silence rather than amend it.
