# 0017 — apps/editor empieza antes de que concluyan las sesiones del prototipo

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Decided by:** the CEO · **Amends:** protocol Part 14, the Fase 1 note

## Context

The Fase 1 note in protocol Part 14 (direction, 22 September 2026) puts two things before
`apps/editor` exists: the detailed screens with all their states, and **a navigable prototype
tested with two real businesses**. The screens were finished on 23 September and reviewed
against the ADRs in `docs/design/REVIEW.md`. The prototype was finished on 24 September, with
each business's three variants generated by the real renderer. **The two sessions have not been
run.**

Meanwhile direction has nothing to touch. Phase 0 closed with a walking skeleton that has no
interface, and everything shown since has been mock-ups. The CEO asked for the real tool
deployed and testable **before 30 September**, and was explicit that the answer is not to build
him something separate to look at: he wants to watch the actual tool grow as features land.

Those two things pull against each other. The gate exists precisely so the editor is not built
on guesses.

## Decision

**`apps/editor` starts now, deployed from its first day, and grows daily.**

The sessions are not cancelled, they are split:
- **the first happens this week**, before the text bank is written, which is the part of the
  gate with the most influence on what gets built in these seven days;
- **the second happens after 30 September**, with the real tool in front of the owner instead
  of the prototype.

## What is preserved

- Both sessions happen, and their findings still amend the ADRs they touch: the text bank
  (0009), the sector list (0010), the photo criteria (0011) and the contact form (0016).
- **The first session lands before the bank is written.** The generated texts are the thing the
  sessions can most cheaply overturn, so that ordering is kept deliberately.
- The Fase 1 acceptance criterion is untouched: someone from outside still has to build a real
  site in under ten minutes, timed and observed. **This week does not deliver that**, and does
  not claim to.

## What is lost, and it is named rather than glossed

- **The guarantee that the editor is built on findings already gathered.** The five
  questionnaire screens are built on day 2 from the approved mock-ups, and the first session
  happens the same day: its write-up will not exist when they are written.
- **The two sessions stop being comparable.** One tests a prototype and the other a product:
  they are two experiments of one subject each, not one observation of two. This is recorded in
  `guion-de-prueba.md` and in ADR 0016, whose reopening condition assumed two answers to the
  same question — so the second session asks the form question word for word.

## Why this is acceptable

What these seven days build is **the generation path**, not the editor's interaction design.
The screens it implements were already checked against every ADR. The parts a session can
overturn most cheaply — the bank's tone, the sector list, the form question — are content and
data, not the application's structure.

## What would reopen this

If the first session shows the **questionnaire itself** is misunderstood — that people look for
their exact trade rather than their sector, or believe question 3's suggestions are already
ticked — that is a finding about the screens, and the screens change before anything else is
built on top of them.

## Consequences

- Protocol Part 14's Fase 1 note gains a line pointing here.
- `apps/editor` exists from 24 September, on Render's free plan, with `noindex` while it is half
  built.

## The other door this sprint crossed, recorded late (26 September 2026)

This ADR is the record of the gates sprint 1 walked through, and one of them was never written
down: **protocol Part 14 lists «descargar» under phase 2, and the ZIP download shipped in phase 1**
— on day 5 of that sprint, and it has been the only way anyone gets a site out ever since.

It is recorded here rather than argued afresh, because the reasoning is the one already in this
ADR: the CEO asked to see the real tool, and a tool nobody can take anything out of is a
demonstration. It is also the half of phase 2's «publicar, descargar» that costs nothing to
give — a file the owner already owns, with no hosting, no domain and no invoice behind it.
«Publicar» stays where it is, and ADR 0008 has it on hold with no plan.

Noted now because [ADR 0018](0018-own-cover-photo-before-phase-2.md) moves another line of the
same list, and an undocumented precedent is worth less than a documented one when the next person
asks whether these boundaries mean anything.
