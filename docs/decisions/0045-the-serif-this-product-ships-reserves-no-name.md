# 0045 — The serif this product ships reserves no name

**Status:** proposed · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Found by** development, checking a claim before
building on it (sprint 24 día 4) · **Decided by: David, 18 de octubre de 2026**, in two answers: to
replace the family rather than rename it or ask for permission, and to **migrate the sites that
already exist** rather than leave them behind · **Recorded by development, which does not sign** ·
**Relates to:** [ADR 0028](0028-typography-needs-a-decision-from-direction.md) (ship the faces),
[ADR 0032](0032-typography-per-element-with-the-faces-that-travel.md),
[ADR 0001](0001-static-published-sites.md) · **Touches:** `packages/renderer`,
`packages/publisher`, `packages/tokens`, `packages/schema`, `apps/editor`, the corpus

## Context

ADR 0028 decided to self-host the faces so a published page shows the letter the owner chose. Two
families were shipped: Inter and Playfair Display, each as its latin woff2 at weights 400 and 700,
with its OFL text beside it.

`packages/publisher/src/fonts.ts` has explained since sprint 14 why the second one could keep its
name:

> **Rule one: the file is the package's file, unmodified.** […] Playfair Display is published **with
> Reserved Font Name "Playfair Display"**, and the SIL Open Font License §3 lets that name travel
> only with an unmodified face […] Shipping it whole is the cheap path *and* the compliant one.

Sprint 24 planned a gallery of shipped faces, and put a verification before it: *if `@fontsource`'s
latin files are subsets, that reasoning does not hold and what we ship today is in question.*

### What the measurement found

**The premise is about the wrong thing.** «Unmodified» there means *we* do not alter the package's
file, which is true and remains true. The licence's test is against the **Original Version**.

- `@fontsource/playfair-display` declares four subsets, and the complete 400-normal face **is four
  files**: cyrillic 11.2 KB, latin 21.3, latin-ext 12.0, vietnamese 5.5. The package's own `400.css`
  carries four `@font-face` rules with four `unicode-range`s. We shipped one of them.
- OFL 1.1, from the licence text that travels beside the face: a «Modified Version» is «any
  derivative made by **adding to, deleting**, or substituting — in part or in whole — any of the
  components of the Original Version, **by changing formats** or by porting the Font Software to a
  new environment». A latin slice in woff2 is both.
- §3: «No Modified Version of the Font Software may use the Reserved Font Name(s) **unless explicit
  written permission is granted** by the corresponding Copyright Holder. This restriction only
  applies to the primary font name as presented to the users.» The renderer presents
  `font-family: 'EB Garamond'` — then, `'Playfair Display'`.

**One family only.** Inter's copyright line declares no reserved name. Playfair Display's declares
one twice. The phrase appears in every OFL's own §3, so the declaration is the copyright line and
nowhere else — a search of the licence body answers yes for every font in existence.

## Decision

### 1. The classic pair's heading face is EB Garamond

Playfair Display leaves `SHIPPABLE_FAMILIES`, the theme stacks, `FONT_SOURCES`, the generated bytes
and the dependency list. EB Garamond takes its place, at the same two weights, as the same kind of
file.

**Chosen against the four filters the gallery will use**, from eight candidates measured on 18
October 2026:

| family | reserves its name | latin | 400 + 700 | name matches the pattern | KB 400 + 700 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EB Garamond** | **no** | yes | yes | yes | **48.0** |
| Libre Baskerville | no | yes | yes | yes | 39.6 |
| Spectral | no | yes | yes | yes | 43.7 |
| Source Serif 4 | no | yes | yes | yes | 40.8 |
| Crimson Pro | no | yes | yes | yes | 36.2 |
| Bitter | no | yes | yes | yes | 36.2 |
| Lora | **yes** | — | — | — | — |
| Merriweather | **yes** | — | — | — | — |

Six passed. **EB Garamond because the pair is called «Clásica y seria»** and a Garamond is the most
classical serif there is, it has a true 700 rather than a synthesised one, and its fallback chain
degrades in character rather than collapsing: `Garamond` is absent on macOS and the next name,
`Baskerville`, was measured present there on 1 October 2026 — an old-style serif after an old-style
serif, where the body's Charter is a transitional one. It costs **48.0 KB against Playfair's 44.0**:
four kilobytes, in a client's ZIP, is the whole price of the change.

### 2. The sites that already exist are migrated, not left behind

A theme stores the **resolved stack**, not the pair it came from, so a site made with «Clásica y
seria» carries `'Playfair Display', Didot, …` in its own bytes. Changing `TYPE_PAIRS` reaches new
sites and no existing one, and the renderer ships a face only when a stack's *first* family is a row
of its table — so leaving those documents alone would not leave them as they were. They would
publish naming a family nobody sends, and their headings would become whatever the visitor happens
to have installed: the exact state ADR 0028 exists to prevent.

**So migration `0012` rewrites that one token**, `SCHEMA_VERSION` 1.10.0 → 1.11.0. It matches the
stack **whole** rather than by prefix — an owner cannot type a family (ADR 0032 §2 makes it
unexpressible), so a heading stack is always one a `TypePair` produced, which makes an exact
comparison both sufficient and the narrowest thing that can be written.

**The letter on those sites changes.** It changes either way; this way it changes to one we ship.

### 3. The filter becomes a refusal, over every row

**No family this product ships may declare a Reserved Font Name.** Asserted in
`packages/publisher/test/fonts.test.ts` over every package in `FONT_SOURCES`, on the copyright line,
together with its being an OFL at all. That is what makes the gallery safe to grow: the condition is
not a thing the next person has to remember.

### 4. The exit that looks obvious is refused, with its number

Shipping «the whole face» — all four subsets per weight — costs **+58.9 KB** (107.4 against 48.5),
which busts the 80 KB bundle budget on its own, **and is still a format conversion**. It does not
answer §3. The only unmodified Original Version is the upstream TTF, which is not a web format this
product uses.

## What is preserved

- **The owner still gets the letter they chose**, on every machine, which is ADR 0028's whole point.
- **Typefaces are still named by character**, never by font, in every screen the owner sees:
  «Clásica y seria» said nothing about which classical serif it was, which is exactly why this
  change can be made without the interface lying.
- **Inter is untouched**, and so are the other two pairs.
- **A face still never travels without its licence**, and `buildSite` still refuses a face without
  one.

## What is lost, and it is named rather than glossed

- **Playfair's didone letterform.** EB Garamond is an old-style serif; the high-contrast display
  character of Playfair is not replaced by it, it is replaced by a different classical one. Sites
  already published with Playfair will look different in their headings after a redownload.
- **Four kilobytes** in every ZIP that uses the classic pair.
- **A reopening condition of ADR 0028 is spent quietly:** its font table named Playfair Display in
  three measured rows, and those rows now describe a family this product does not ship. The table
  stays as the dated measurement it was.

## What this does not decide

- **The gallery.** Which further families to offer, named by their real names, is the next day's
  work and its own decision.
- **Whether to ask Playfair Display's authors for the written permission §3 provides for.** It
  remains available, costs nothing in bytes, and would let the family come back as one gallery row
  among others. David declined to make it the remedy; he did not refuse the request.
- **Windows.** ADR 0028's table still has «not measured» where Windows should be, and
  `fixtures/font-check.html` is still the procedure.

## Consequences

- `packages/renderer`: one row of `SHIPPABLE_FAMILIES`.
- `packages/tokens`: `classic-display`'s heading stack, and the note that carried the old
  measurement.
- `packages/publisher`: `FONT_SOURCES`, the regenerated `fontData.ts` (139.7 KB), the package's own
  dependency and the comment that carried the wrong reasoning.
- `packages/schema`: migration `0012`, `SCHEMA_VERSION` 1.11.0, the snapshot, five round-trip tests.
- **Two goldens move** — `menu-y-paginas` and `menu-curado`, the only two fixtures on this pair —
  and the diff is the family name and the two file names, nothing else.
- `apps/editor`: the `fonts/[file]` route grows its row by construction, and every mention of the
  old family in prose and in walks is corrected rather than left to be found.
- The record is `docs/design/REVIEW.md`, sprint 24.
