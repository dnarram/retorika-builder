## The phase 1 acceptance criterion

The protocol states it as:

> «Una persona de fuera del equipo, sin explicación previa y sin ayuda, monta la web de un negocio
> real en menos de diez minutos. Se cronometra y se observa, no se pregunta.»

Every clause of it held, and each is recorded separately rather than as one verdict:

- **From outside the team** — a real business owner, not anyone who has seen this being built.
- **No prior explanation, no help** — «El cliente no hizo preguntas y usó correctamente la app.»
  Nothing was demonstrated and nothing was explained, which is what the script forbids.
- **A real business** — her own.
- **Under ten minutes** — eight, to the downloaded ZIP.
- **Timed and observed, not asked** — the questions came afterwards, as the script requires.

**And the same session says she would not publish the result as it stands.** Both facts are true,
neither cancels the other, and putting only one of them in a summary would be choosing which half
to report. The criterion as written asks whether someone can *build* a site unaided in under ten
minutes. It does not ask whether they are satisfied with it. She could, in eight; she is not, yet.

**Whether that counts as phase 1 accepted is the CEO's to say, not development's.** What is
recorded here is the measurement.

## Contact → ADR 0016

**Asked word for word.** The question, as it stands in the script and in ADR 0016:

> «Cuando alguien quiere pediros cita o preguntar algo, ¿cómo te gusta que lo haga? ¿Te vale con
> que te llamen o te escriban por WhatsApp, o esperas que te escriban desde la web y que te llegue
> a ti?»

Her answer, quoted:

> «Me gusta que me reserven enviando mensajes a whatssap, enviando un email, rellenando un
> formulario muy simple desde la web con la información justa o llamando por teléfono.»

**This fires ADR 0016's reopening condition.** That condition reads: «If either of the two says
they expect messages to arrive from the web, this is reopened». The question offers two halves —
"is it enough that they call or WhatsApp you" against "do you expect them to write from the web
and have it reach you" — and she did not take the first half alone. She named **a form on the
website**, in her own words, as one of the four ways she would like to be reserved with.

Three things about that answer deserve to survive into whatever decides this next, because they
are the difference between "a form" and "the form she asked for":

- **She qualified it twice**: «muy simple» and «con la información justa». She is not asking for a
  contact form with eight fields. Whatever a phase 2 decision builds, that qualifier is evidence
  about its size.
- **It was third of four**, and the other three — WhatsApp, email, phone — are exactly what ADR
  0016 already ships. The decision as built serves most of what she said. The form is an addition
  to it, not a replacement for it.
- **Which is precisely what ADR 0016 predicted.** Its own words: «the links work whether or not a
  form is ever added, so if the sessions reopen this, a form is an addition in phase 2 rather than
  a rewrite». The foresight held: nothing that shipped has to be undone.

**Both owners named a form.** Taberna's answer is not admissible — that question was paraphrased —
but he said «formulario» spontaneously too. One admissible answer and one inadmissible one, both
pointing the same way, is still one admissible answer; it is recorded here because it is the
honest shape of the evidence, not because it adds up to two.

## The five closing questions

Asked after the download, as the script requires against the real product.

1. **«¿Publicarías esto tal y como está?»**

   > «Necesita trabajo visual, no puedo poner negritas o cambiar estilos ni mover imágenes por
   > donde quiera…»

   A no, with the reason attached. Three distinct gaps in one sentence, and they are not the same
   kind of thing — see "What the reason breaks down into" below.

2. **«¿Qué le falta?»**

   > «Falta elementos visuales: colores, la posibilidad de mover libremente las imágenes o de
   > editar los textos con opciones gráficas avanzadas de negrita, subrayado, etc. En general falta
   > impacto visual tal y como está ahora mismo. Además la página se muestra de manera continua y a
   > mí me gustaría que la página web constara de varias páginas independientes, que se pudiera
   > navegar dentro de ella. Me gustaría también una sección para mostrar mis platos, la carta,
   > etc.»

3. **«¿Qué le sobra?»** — «No veo que le sobre nada.»

   The same answer Taberna gave, word for word in substance. **Two out of two found nothing
   superfluous**, which is the one thing both sessions agreed on without qualification. A generated
   site is not padded.

4. **«¿Estos textos suenan a los tuyos?»**

   > «Creo que no, me gustarían textos más enfocados a la hostelería. En hostelería es muy
   > importante convencer y hacer soñar con comidas y servicio. Hacer un poco soñar con el plato.»

5. **«Si esto costase un pago único, ¿por cuánto te parecería bien?»** — «50-100 euros.»

## What the reason breaks down into

Answers 1 and 2 are the substance of the session, and they contain four different requests that
have four different homes. Separating them matters, because three are already-planned phase 2 work
and one collides with a foundational rule.

- **Colours and styles** — «colores», «cambiar estilos». This is `Estilo`, which protocol Part 14
  puts in phase 2 and which the editor's rail already draws dimmed with a `Fase 2` badge. She felt
  the gap the badge describes. Nothing to decide: it is scheduled.
- **Several pages with navigation** — «que la página web constara de varias páginas independientes,
  que se pudiera navegar dentro de ella». This is `Páginas`, phase 2, also dimmed in the rail. Also
  scheduled. Worth noting that she read the one-page site as a deficiency rather than a style, and
  that no owner has yet been shown a multi-page result to compare it against.
- **Bold, underline, "advanced graphic options" for text** — this one is **not** scheduled, and it
  is not small. A text element's value is `{ kind: "text", text: string }`: a plain string, with no
  inline markup anywhere in the model. Bold inside a sentence is a schema change, a migration, a
  renderer change and a decision about what an "editable text" is — and ADR 0004's role vocabulary
  is closed. It would need its own ADR before any code.
- **Moving images freely** — «mover libremente las imágenes o de editar... por donde quiera».
  **This collides head-on with document rule 4**, which says positions are relative to the
  section's grid and never absolute on the page, and with rule 1, which keeps layout from owning
  content. The rule exists so a site cannot be broken into an unpublishable mess and so mobile can
  be derived rather than hand-built (rule 7). One owner wanting free positioning does not overturn
  a foundation — but it is the first time a real user has pushed against rule 4, and that is worth
  having on the record rather than discovering again later. `CLAUDE.md` is explicit about what
  acting on it would take: «If a task seems to require breaking one, the task is wrong. Write an
  ADR before touching code.»

## The menu, asked for twice → a naming finding, not only a missing section

> «Me gustaría también una sección para mostrar mis platos, la carta, etc.»

Taberna asked for the same thing, unprompted, in the first session: «le falta una sección para
mostrar los platos estrella o poner la carta del restaurante».

**Two owners out of two asked for it.** Both are hostelería, so this is evidence about that sector
rather than about every business — a hairdresser has not asked for a carta and would not.

The more useful half is the naming. The concept dossier's nine sections already include
**«Precios»** (`docs/tasks/catalog-que-hago.md` names the five list sections: Qué hago, Fotos de
trabajos, Opiniones, Precios, Equipo). A restaurant's carta *is* a price list. So the section both
owners want may already be planned — under a name neither of them used or would recognise.
Whoever builds it should decide whether "Precios" is the name a restaurant owner reads as "la
carta", and the answer from two sessions is that it is not.

## Her words → ADR 0009

The script asks for the owner's own vocabulary, because that is what the text bank should be using:
«hostelería», «convencer», «hacer soñar con comidas y servicio», «hacer soñar con el plato»,
«platos», «la carta».

**Two out of two said the texts do not sound like theirs**, and they said it differently, which is
worth keeping apart rather than averaging:

- **Taberna** wanted them *longer and more explanatory* — «los míos serían más explicativos, un
  poco más extensos» — and found the page «vacía y fría».
- **Conchi** wants them *more evocative* — the bank describes a service where hostelería needs to
  sell an experience.

They are not the same complaint. What they share is that the register is wrong for hostelería, and
that is now the clearest finding either session produced about ADR 0009.

## The questionnaire → ADR 0010 and ADR 0017

**No questions, no hesitation, used correctly.** «El cliente no hizo preguntas y usó correctamente
la app.» Two minutes, five screens.

That is the second session in a row to find the questionnaire understood, and it closes the
reopening condition ADR 0017 wrote for itself: «If the first session shows the **questionnaire
itself** is misunderstood … the screens change before anything else is built on top of them.»
Neither session showed that. The gate ADR 0017 crossed is now evidenced by both.
